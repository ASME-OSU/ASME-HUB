# Officer transition: start here

Start in the [Officer Hub](https://asme-osu.github.io/ASME-HUB/) → **Transition to New Year**. The year choice stays at the top on every step: enter the starting year (for example, **2027** for 2027–2028) and choose **Use year**. Choose **Practice** or **Real handoff**, then **Start checklist**, or choose a saved checklist for that year and **Continue checklist**. A checklist is one attempt, with its own notes and private links. Naming is optional. The identifier, reload and step-jump controls stay collapsed under **Checklist details and advanced controls**.

Use **Choose checklist** at the top to select the attempt. In the middle, follow the current step's tasks and choose your observations under **Record results**. At the bottom, **Saved links and backup tools** belongs to that same checklist: open **Saved links** for tool and document URLs, or **Progress backup and print** for results and notes. Step 2's **Edit saved links** button opens the bottom editor; it is the same list on every step.

Ask the President through [asme@osu.edu](mailto:asme@osu.edu) for the filled current five-step checklist, private handoff folder, technical setup information, setup receipt and private link-bundle JSON for your year and attempt. Include your role, year and checklist purpose. For example: “I am the incoming Secretary, preparing 2027–2028 in Practice. Please send the current checklist, copied-tool links and setup receipt, and explain the review and recovery steps.” If these are missing, mark Blocked; you can still read later steps and do local calendar practice. A shared start page or dated example folder does not supply your current materials.

The President leads, with the team contributing checks. Record the supplied folder/checklist links, technical/recovery contact and one settings editor in the private checklist. Practice uses the approved test account and reviewer; Real handoff needs each incoming holder's actual account access. Check existing annual folders and copies with the maintainer before creating more.

The Secretary/Points officer reviews the annual tool bundle, manages approved inputs/Form choices and tests results. The technical maintainer creates annual copies and verifies installed helpers, import wiring and recovery. For the two response checks, the officer matches the five fictional submissions in the named response tab; the maintainer inspects the other response tabs in that copied workbook and supplies a dated result. Neither check certifies every external destination. The Treasurer checks fictional arithmetic in Practice and real funding/bank evidence in Real handoff. The Webmaster checks calendars, backups and approved public communications. Use the [current implementation and verification status](current-implementation.md) for exact scope and dated native observations.

This folder contains public documentation. Keep private configuration, exact operational file inventories, test/member records and evidence in the chapter-controlled private handoff folder. If you cannot find it, ask the outgoing President for its location; do not assume a dated example folder is the current handoff.

For a new attempt, the President copies and fills the [five-step private checklist template](private-run-checklist-template.md) and [selective practice cleanup template](selective-mock-cleanup-template.md) in that private folder. The maintainer supplies a current setup receipt: a readable list of the copied files and checked settings. The `readableReceipt` formatter is one possible source only after its installed version is verified. The President coordinates technical help, recovery and review and records decisions. Record the contact as **the President**; a personal name is not required. These public templates contain no current private links or approvals.

Use **Add a note or evidence** for a sentence such as “I opened the folder, but could not edit Points Master. The President will arrange access.” A screenshot or private record link helps when someone must compare a setting, calculation or approval later. Private record fields take full HTTPS document/folder links supplied by the President or maintainer, not names or email addresses. Practice uses **Reviewer sign-off record link** and **Recovery notes document link**. Approved communications and coordinator launch-approval fields appear only in Real handoff. **Import automation links** accepts the maintainer's annual link-handoff JSON for this year and attempt; it does not run setup.

Start an actual transition in the [Officer Hub](https://asme-osu.github.io/ASME-HUB/)
under **Transition to New Year**. Select the year above the checklist controls and work through the
five steps: T01 Confirm access and materials; T02 Get the annual tool bundle and check it; T03 Run check-in and budget tests; T04 Check calendars and prepare communications; T05 Close the rehearsal or launch an approved year (including cleanup/handoff after NO-GO). A saved inactive settings row is one milestone; it does not mean
the new year is activated or every handoff check passed.
