# Officer handoff: start here

Open the [Officer Hub](https://asme-osu.github.io/ASME-HUB/) → **Transition to New Year → Open transition guide**. Work through the five steps in order. You can read later steps without marking them complete. Real handoff has **20 applicable reviews** (1/4/7/5/3 by step); Practice has **16** (1/4/7/2/2). Reviews are manual observations: saved links, examples and downloads do not pass them.

## 1. Choose your year

Enter the first year, such as **2027** for 2027–2028, choose **Real handoff** or **Practice**, then **Continue to access check**. Confirm the chapter account and correct officer or Practice folder using the checkbox, then **Continue to file setup**. A completed saved checklist can be reopened with **Resume saved progress**. Other attempts, notes and troubleshooting are under **Help, notes and saved progress**. Your progress and the private **Officer checklist document** are separate records.

No standard tool links need to be pasted in Step 1. Annual Setup saves the new Points Master, check-in form and budget connections; the Hub uses that year's settings automatically when the approved year becomes current. Shared tools such as Newsletter Builder, Social Hub and the officer workspace keep their existing links. Additional resources are optional and managed separately in Resources.

Sign in with **asmeohiostate@gmail.com**, which owns the chapter Google files. Open the shared officer workspace and private checklist. Use your normal approved OSU login for Microsoft/shared university resources where required. Routine access is a login check; no separate reviewer acceptance is needed. Record missing permissions with the owner and next action. The **Annual Setup access check is in Step 2**, where its link and profile instructions are available. Annual files are created or reopened there.

### Words used here

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Control Center | The chapter settings spreadsheet. Practice uses a separate copied settings spreadsheet. |
| Export | A separate workbook containing approved results from a working master. It is not automatically public or approved for sharing. |
| Readiness record | Dated evidence identifying the owner, files, checks and observed results. |
| Inactive / current | Inactive means not enabled; current means the year selected by the chapter tools. These are separate settings. |
| Suppression | Hiding member rows from an export while preserving private working records. |
| Consumer | A tool using shared results or settings: the Hub, website, Form or newsletter. Each needs its own check. |
| Ledger / projected ledger | Current approved cash balance / balance including pending entries. Neither proves bank reconciliation. |
| Preheader | The preview line beside a newsletter subject in an inbox. |
| JSON | A text backup containing named fields that the tool can import. |

## 2. Prepare this year’s files

Choose **Sign in with chapter Google account** in Step 2. Confirm the approved chapter account in Google’s sign-in window, check **Member goal for this year**, then choose **Create or reopen this year’s files**. Each member counts once; use the President’s approved goal. An existing year keeps its original goal.

One designated operator creates or reopens files for the selected year and purpose. Real handoff and Practice must use separate approved workspaces. Use **Open files for review** for the main workbooks, Form and Control Center; **Help and recovery → All saved files and reference links** holds the full list. Existing files can be reopened with **Help and recovery → Reopen setup**.

**If connected setup is unavailable:** open **Help and recovery → Detailed instructions & troubleshooting → Manual fallback** and **More help for this step → Open Annual Setup — manual fallback**. For this external page, use Chrome’s ASME profile with **asmeohiostate@gmail.com as its only signed-in Google account**. Confirm the year, purpose and goal. Choose **Copy setup results**, return to the Hub, paste into **Paste setup results**, then **Save setup results**. Downloads are optional backups.

The four **Confirm your file review** checkboxes record your own observations. They do not change Google settings. For another result, a skip reason or notes, open **Help and recovery**. Saved-link editing and separate backup tools are also there.

## 3. Check attendance and finances

### Real handoff

Review actual event settings, approved finances, bank reconciliation and both export imports. Use existing evidence or agree separate Practice files for exercises. Do not enter fictional attendance or transactions in real working files. Record file or import errors as Blocked with the exact message. ## 5. Approve and start the year

### Real handoff — Approve and start the year

Real activation requires incoming acceptance, required reviews, actual funding/reconciliation and coordinator launch approval. Keep a separate launch rollback plan identifying previous year/settings, before-change snapshot, named restore operator, exact restore actions, failure/stop criteria and decision/contact route.

At the approved time, only the authorized settings owner sets the verified new row **is_active = TRUE**, **is_current = TRUE** and Points **Config!B5 = LIVE**. Set the previous year’s **is_current = FALSE**, preserving its records. Do not clear its active flag unless the approved plan requires that. Check the intended intake state and recheck **Hub, website, Form and newsletter**. An unresolved review means no launch; record the missing item, owner and next action.

### Practice only — Close and review your practice run

End with Points **TESTING**, Form intake closed, all event `form_open` FALSE and settings inactive/noncurrent. Preserve evidence or perform reviewed cleanup and record the practice result and its limits; never activate Practice files. After all required Practice steps and checks pass, choose **Finish practice**. This records the no-launch reason automatically and preserves your notes.

For MOCK attendance cleanup, use **ASME Tools → Clear Test Data** only after the owner approves the reviewed disposition. This optional copied-menu action can request its own Google script consent. It requires the exact annual Form/tab and closed TESTING state, verifies a private archive, then removes reviewed response IDs/raw rows. Original Google Form response IDs cannot be restored. Cancel to retain evidence. Event definitions, adjustments and budget inputs need separate input-cell review; preserve formulas. Mixed real/unmarked attendance stops cleanup. If the menu is missing, reload once; older copies may not have this action because template code does not propagate to existing copies; retain their data and record the limitation.

## Progress and starting again

The progress bar counts passed reviews and completed steps. Select **Yes — checked** only for actual observations. When tasks and applicable reviews are done, **Save and continue** saves the step and opens the next one. **Preview next step** only reads ahead; it does not complete work. Failed saves stay on the current step.

Step 4 groups **Check events**, **Preview newsletter** and **Save backups**. **Open backup tools** expands progress and private-link backups; the editable newsletter draft is backed up separately in Newsletter Builder.

Step 5 starts with a readiness summary. **See remaining items** expands the applicable reviews; **Go to next unfinished step** returns to earlier work. **Finish handoff** requires production approvals and records the handoff; the authorized owner follows the approved launch plan. **Finish practice** requires all Practice steps, cleanup and recorded results, and never launches a real year.

To start again, open **Step 1 → More options → Checklist options → Reset checklist progress**, review the year and attempt, then **Start fresh checklist**. This carries saved links but starts without results or notes. Previous progress remains under **More options → Other saved progress**. Unsaved edits do not carry over. No Google files are created by resetting progress.
