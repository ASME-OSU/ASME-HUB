## T02 Get this year's tools

- [ ] In Hub Step 2, choose Sign in with chapter Google account as asmeohiostate@gmail.com. Confirm the year, approved goal and workspace purpose, then one operator chooses Create or reopen this year’s files. Reuse the same year/workspace after interruption; teammates reuse those files.

Review actual destination, imports, dates and inactive/noncurrent settings. A report does not certify all source inputs/scoring, personal access or approval; repository source does not prove Google installation.

## T03 Prepare attendance and finances

- [ ] Points officer reviews intended attendance operation, member results and unresolved exceptions.
- [ ] Open Points Export and Budget Export, select any Google import error cell and choose Allow access only for the matching annual source; wait, then Check connections. Inspect unexplained old/test inputs without clearing retained records. Compare Form choices after an approved Events edit in Google Sheets; Connected and observed delivery are separate states.
- [ ] Treasurer reviews real approved funding, balances, pending transactions and bank reconciliation.
- [ ] Assign any unresolved technical or financial work. Keep a persistent error unresolved with its exact message. Any optional scoring exercise belongs in separate Practice copies.

## T04 Update calendars and communications

- [ ] Confirm calendar/newsletter owners, approved sources and upcoming dates.
- [ ] Prepare communications with reviewed facts, sender details and approval.
- [ ] Keep separate backups of Hub progress, private links and editable Newsletter Draft. Restore and compare the three files in a separate persistent browser profile while preserving original work. Record the actual year, seven links, saved note and editable newsletter comparisons; downloads alone do not pass recovery.

## T05 Approve and switch to the new year

- [ ] Record incoming acceptance, remaining blockers and their owners.
- [ ] Record the President's handoff decision and separate launch approval.
- [ ] Authorized operator records any approved switch, readback and launch rollback plan: previous year/settings, named restore operator, exact restore actions, failure/stop criteria and decision/contact route. An inactive saved row alone is not activation.
- [ ] At approved activation time, settings owner sets verified new row is_active/is_current TRUE and Points Config!B5 LIVE, sets previous year is_current FALSE preserving records, and rechecks Hub, website, Form and newsletter. Do not clear previous is_active unless the approved plan requires it.
- [ ] Agree outgoing access changes after incoming acceptance.
- [ ] If retiring an old managed event connection, close its Form and make its row noncurrent first. Check retired-year connection, then Open event update controls. Confirm that exact year and choose Stop event updates on the Google page. If a pending-stop journal is reported, confirm Finish stopping updates there; do not delete unrelated triggers.

## Practice only — Close and review your practice run

If a separate practice is agreed, record the chapter account, separate private copies, operator and purpose. Choose scenarios appropriate to the uncertainty: for example attendance delivery/scoring, fictional budget arithmetic, calendar previews or three-file recovery. The documented five-case scenario is one possible practice plan, not a requirement for every officer or real handoff.

Before adding fictional inputs, inventory existing retained evidence and decide whether to inspect it or run new controlled tests. Record actual results, limits and keep/remove decisions in the [selective practice cleanup record](selective-mock-cleanup-template.md). Practice acceptance never supplies real incoming-account access, funding approval or launch authority.

Keep Points TESTING, Form intake closed, event form_open FALSE and settings inactive/noncurrent. Record activation Skipped — Rehearsal only, your practice result, limits and retention/cleanup disposition. A separate Practice reviewer is not required. Recovery notes describe the Practice three-file restore; they do not approve a production rollback or launch.
