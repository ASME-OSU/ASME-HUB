# Practice restoring your three backups

Use a separate browser profile so your working checklist and newsletter stay intact. A private or Incognito window is not a persistent recovery workspace. The President names the reviewer and supplies the current checklist, copied-tool receipt and link bundle. Do not use another year's tools just to complete this exercise.

## Download from your working profile

1. In the Hub, use **Choose checklist** at the top to open your chosen year's attempt. At the bottom, under **Saved links and backup tools**, expand **Progress backup and print** and choose **Export progress**. The download is a `.json` file containing the year, checklist name/mode, step results and notes.
2. Under **Saved links and backup tools**, expand **Saved links**; the heading also shows the chosen year. Save and review the links, then choose **Download private link bundle**. This is a second `.json` file: progress alone does not contain the copied-tool links or record URLs.
3. In Newsletter Builder, open **Templates → Export Draft .json**. Keep this editable draft, not just an HTML export. Do not choose New Issue or Reset during backup.
4. Find all three files in Downloads. Open each with TextEdit or another plain-text editor; do not edit or save over them. Check progress `year`, `run.name`, `run.mode`, `steps` and notes; link bundle `year`, `links` and record URLs; newsletter `kind`, `version`, `exportedAt` and `state`. Compare them with the screens you just exported. Keep private files in the chapter-approved storage location; do not post their content on GitHub.

## Restore in a separate Chrome profile

5. Open Chrome's profile menu at the top right → **Add** → **Continue without an account** (wording may vary). Name it **ASME recovery practice**. Use the same official Hub and Newsletter Builder addresses as your working profile. Unlock the Hub with your usual authorized dashboard password; Google tool access still requires the approved chapter account. Do not change sharing to make the test easier.
6. In the Hub, select the same target year and Practice mode. Under **Progress backup and print → Import progress**, select the downloaded progress file. Import creates a separate checklist rather than replacing the original. Check its year, mode, steps and notes.
7. In that imported checklist's **Saved links**, choose **Import automation links** and select the separate link-bundle JSON. This fills a draft; click **Save private link list on this device** to save it. Compare all seven tool links, optional copied Control Center and record links with the receipt. Saving changed links intentionally reopens affected checks; recovery is successful when the correct data returns, not when every result still says Passed. Confirm only observations you actually rechecked.
8. In Newsletter Builder's new profile, choose **Templates → Import Draft .json**, select the newsletter backup and compare subject, preheader, content, target year and links in Desktop/Mobile previews. Confirm an editable field is available; do not send or change the working profile's draft.
9. Return to your working profile and confirm the original checklist and newsletter remain intact. Keep the backups and write a short note: **I restored the three files in ASME recovery practice. The year, tool links, notes and newsletter content matched. [Reviewer] checked this on [date].** Record any mismatch as Blocked and ask the President/technical maintainer to help. Never mark recovery Passed just because a download button worked.

For calendar practice, enter the full year, for example **2027-2028**. After **Remove fictional events**, select **Upcoming** in Newsletter Builder or **Upcoming dates** in the public page's generated list. The fictional label must disappear and chapter status return. **Refresh chapter snapshot** retries a failed load. The embedded Google calendar is a separate live view; this practice creates no Google events or invitations.
